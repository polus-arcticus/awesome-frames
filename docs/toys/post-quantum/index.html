Post-quantum: a survey pass

Prompted by a Vitalik Buterin post surveying post-quantum cryptography families — Falcon, ML-DSA, "some other lattice-based thing," something code-based or isogeny-based, "even more esoteric." Three scoping decisions shaped this pass, all deliberate:

  1. Off-chain/JS only. PQC building blocks (NTT, lattice sampling, isogeny walks, syndrome decoding) mostly have no EVM precompile analog, unlike RSA/EC which map onto MODEXP/ecrecover. No Solidity, no Yul, no EIP-8141 framing this round.
  2. A survey, not one deep build. Six concrete schemes, each scoped to what's actually achievable and honest at survey depth — not six half-finished attempts at full production-grade implementations.
  3. Concrete picks over vague families — including discovering @noble/post-quantum (implements ML-KEM/FIPS 203, ML-DSA/FIPS 204, SLH-DSA/FIPS 205, Falcon) exists and can serve as real ground truth, the same role @noble/curves already plays for BIP-340.

Residents, with an honest status column​

SchemeFamilyStatusWhy
ML-KEMlattice (Module-LWE)Full buildNo floating point, no rejection-sampling loop — the most tractable lattice scheme here, and a key-exchange/signature contrast with ML-DSA mirroring ToyCurveECDH (DH) vs. YoloRSA/BIP-340 (signing) elsewhere in this repo.
Lamporthash-based ("esoteric")Full buildGenuinely elementary — no algebraic structure, no external ground truth needed. Includes a real forgery demo.
Falconlattice (NTRU + GPV)Verify-onlySigning needs floating-point Gaussian sampling — a real, documented hazard (part of why FIPS 206 is still Draft). Verification is pure integer arithmetic.
ML-DSAlattice (Module-LWE+SIS)ConceptualReal parameters, no working code — Fiat-Shamir-with-Aborts, bit decomposition, and hint-bit compression are real extra complexity beyond ML-KEM, deserving their own pass rather than a rushed one.
Classic McEliececode-basedFull build (a stretch goal that landed)A real toy binary Goppa code — GF(16) arithmetic, an actual Goppa parity-check construction, real S/P scrambling — decoded via a brute-force syndrome lookup table standing in for Patterson's algorithm.
Isogeny (CSIDH design + the SIDH break)isogeny-basedDocs-onlyReal isogeny arithmetic (Vélu's formulas, extension-field curves) is a research-grade lift — not a survey-pass item. The design and the 2022 SIDH collapse are worth the write-up regardless.

(Multivariate — Rainbow, broken by Beullens in 2022 — was considered and deferred entirely; a good future case study, comparable in weight to McEliece/isogeny.)

Why this belongs in toys, not tools​

The dividing line: nothing here is wired into a deployable EIP-8141 account. MlKem is the odd one out in a good way — real FIPS 203 parameters, full security, nothing shrunk — but it's still a toy rather than a tool because there's no account attached to it (yet) and no on-chain framing this pass. Everything else is incomplete or shrunk on purpose too: ML-DSA and isogeny are deliberately unfinished, Falcon is verify-only because the missing half is genuinely hazardous, Lamport shrinks the bit count signed (not the preimages themselves) for legibility, and McEliece's n=15 is nowhere near the 3488+ a real deployment needs. None of that is a defect to fix later within this pass; it's the honest shape of a survey.
